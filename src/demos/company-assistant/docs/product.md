# Nimbus — product overview

Nimbus is a weather-alert service for farms. Sensors in the field send readings every 5 minutes;
Nimbus predicts frost and heavy rain 6–12 hours ahead and texts the farmer.

- Plans: Starter $29/month (up to 10 sensors), Pro $99/month (up to 100 sensors, API access).
- Stack: TypeScript services on Node.js, PostgreSQL, a forecasting model retrained weekly.
- Biggest customer region: Galilee, Israel; second: Emilia-Romagna, Italy.
