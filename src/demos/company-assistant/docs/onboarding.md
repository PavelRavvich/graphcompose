# Onboarding at Nimbus Labs

## First week

- Day 1: laptop, accounts (GitHub, Slack, Linear), lunch with your buddy.
- Day 2–3: run the product locally, read the architecture overview in `product.md`.
- Day 4: pair with your buddy on a small bug from the `good-first-issue` label.
- Day 5: demo what you learned at the Friday show-and-tell (10 minutes, no slides needed).

## Rules of thumb

- Ask in #help before spending more than 30 minutes stuck.
- Every change goes through a pull request with one review.
- On-call starts after your third month.
